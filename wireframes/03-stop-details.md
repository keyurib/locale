# Screen 3 — Stop details

## Purpose
Explain why a specific stop was recommended and help the guest decide whether to add or reserve it.

## Layout
- Back button at the top.
- Large image placeholder.
- Content below in a clean, readable column.
- Primary action near the bottom.

## Content

### Header
- Back label: **Back to Locale**
- Large rounded image placeholder at the top.

### Stop information
- Stop name: **House of MG Café**
- Category: **Café**
- Neighborhood: **Manek Chowk**

### Why this fits
Use this explanation:

> A relaxed indoor stop that fits your rainy weather, ₹1,500 budget, and interest in food and local character.

### Practical details
Show as a simple list or detail rows:
- Opening hours: Open until 10:00 PM
- Reservation: Recommended
- Walking time from stay: 12 minutes
- Estimated spend: ₹350
- Neighborhood: Manek Chowk

### Alternatives
Show two compact alternative cards:
1. The Project Café
2. Zen Cafe

Each alternative should include:
- Small image placeholder
- Name
- Category
- Estimated cost
- Distance

### Actions
- Primary CTA: **Add to Locale**
- Secondary CTA: **Back to plan**

## Interaction notes
- “Add to Locale” can show a success state such as “Added to your Locale.”
- “Back to plan” returns to Screen 2.
- Keep this screen focused on one stop; do not turn it into a full search results page.
